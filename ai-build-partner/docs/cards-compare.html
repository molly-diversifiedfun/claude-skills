THE VERDICT

SVGs: fast, inline, zero API cost. but no soul. placeholder energy.

v1 Generated: pretty, on-brand, professional. your mom would frame these. but would you screenshot them?

v2 Meme: the ones people actually send to friends. "three sprints in a trenchcoat" is the kind of line that gets screenshotted. the cat in the empty room. the flame on the couch watching netflix. THIS is the energy.

Recommendation: v2 meme prompts locked as the canonical card generation style. v1 is too safe. the whole point of Unstuck is "smart friend two drinks in" — the cards should match.
